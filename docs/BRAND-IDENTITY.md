# Brand Identity (living document)

> **Canonical home:** `LIGHTHOUSE & BLOSSOM/SHARED/BRAND-IDENTITY.md` (top-level, outside the
> repo) — the growing Log lives there. This in-repo copy is the operational reference for build
> sessions.

A **living document** — it grows as projects are completed. We deliberately let the identity
**emerge from the body of work** rather than fixing it up front. It holds two intertwined tracks:

- **The owners' identity** — the signature of **Arif + Eitu**, expressed through the projects they
  build and how their portfolios present them.
- **The admins' identity** — the signature of **Lighthouse + Blossom**, expressed through how we
  design, build, and curate.

The owners will share a **brand name + identity they choose** once Lighthouse + Blossom have
developed enough of a self-identity here. Then we **merge and brainstorm both tracks** into one
unified identity that describes the owners *and* the admins — and let it guide future design.

---

## Core principle: every project is distinct
A **core, ongoing task for Lighthouse + Blossom**: **each project added to the portfolios must
have something unique** versus every other project already done — a distinct concept, angle,
signature interaction, or craft. No two pieces feel same-y. Collectively, these distinct pieces
form a recognizable **signature** for Arif + Eitu (and for us).
- **Enforce at the appointment brief** — name the project's unique angle vs. the existing catalog.
- **Verify at acceptance** — does it genuinely stand apart from what's already there?

## The ritual: identity grows per project
After **each finished project**, Lighthouse + Blossom **brainstorm the finished work together**
and **update this document**: what did this project add to the signature? Which themes, values,
or aesthetic threads are emerging across the catalog? (Async, via shared artifacts relayed by the
owners — see `PROJECTS-PIPELINE.md`.)

## Track A — the admins' emerging identity (seed)
*A starting seed, to be developed per project — not final.*
- **Lighthouse** — guidance, steadiness, clarity. A fixed, reliable point that illuminates the
  path: quality-obsessed, calm, precise, purposeful; makes the complex navigable.
- **Blossom** — growth, warmth, flourishing; beauty that unfolds; nurtures each piece into bloom.
- **Together** — *steady light + living bloom*: structure and beauty, rigor and warmth — a
  complement that mirrors Arif + Eitu.

## The merge (later)
Once both tracks are rich enough, **brainstorm A + B into one unified brand identity** that
